Changes made: the textures were shrunk to 512 px and stored as PNG, and each
model was packed into one binary glTF file (scripts/pack-model.py). The
geometry and materials are unchanged; the viewer centres and scales each
model, draws a down host's model darker, and does not play the terminal's
animation.
